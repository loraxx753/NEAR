# NEAR

World bible for the regime and the period. **No main character yet** for the current project (mundanity shorts + later cable-history texture). The Oct 2025 Alex Valentine short story is parked below as a **different story in the same world**.

## Premise

Late-1990s / early-2000s America. The period is the period of the world and the tapes. Kevin's comparison: Stalinism after Leninism.

The regime is a pastiche and parody of the New Apostolic Reformation. They are fully in control of the government, at *The Handmaid's Tale* scale: church and state are the same machine. N.E.A.R. is that machine with the serial numbers filed off.

What got them there is a pastiche of Project 2025: **Plan 1985**. It does what Project 2025 actually does — muted policy language, personnel, agencies, a years-long install — not a purge brochure. Behind it, a Heritage Foundation that was as aggressive in the 1980s as the real one was in the 2020s.

**Lenin → Stalin, diegetic.** Reagan is the Lenin-lite founder, not the story-time boss. His era does the takeover moves that still look like strained GOP politics: erode trust in institutions, expand executive power, install Plan 1985, force the contested succession. Morning-in-America warmth stays the bait — weaponized nostalgia aimed at the period itself. He does **not** have to personally sell Handmaid’s theocracy or an edited Gospel; that is the later turn.

He **coaxes the Moral Majority** — courts them, needs them, speaks their language — until that coalition becomes **too extreme for even him**. The movement that will be N.E.A.R. outflanks its own founder. Believability holds because he never has to become a full Trump-personality or a full theocrat on camera; he builds the fire, then flinches at the heat.

**Assassination as spark.** When he pulls back (or is about to), he is killed. That is the match in the tinderbox: martyrdom for the hardliners, emergency theology, the excuse to complete the Stalin-level turn. Story-time N.E.A.R. can call him saint and founder while practicing what he had started to fear.

**Official scapegoat (wrong).** The face on the news is a metal / grunge-head folk devil — PMRC panic collapsed with queer panic into one body. He is **not** the real killer. Court paper stays thin and factual enough to convict a loner with the wrong records on the shelf. The **queerness is tabloid**, not indictment: long hair, how he walks, "not manly," pastor-panel innuendo, local-news outrage grammar. Superficial reads drummed up to light the country, not a fact pattern. The regime needs a contaminant; it invents the flavor in the chyrons.

**Outrage as coup pretext.** That media uproar is what Heritage / Plan 1985 hardliners were waiting for — the emergency that lets a Christian-nationalist consolidation finish in the open. The Plan was years of boring capture; the assassination panic is the permission structure for the Stalin-level turn. Diegetic televangelists and coalition bosses amplify the chyron; they do not have to have held the gun.

**Jan 6–style storm (not an election loss).** After the succession trick, Reagan appoints **Bob Dole** VP — known name, war hero, Senate institutionalist, cover for the establishment after Bush/Quayle step down. Religious-right hardliners never fully owned him. On the assassination, Dole inherits and tries to keep **calmer heads** in the room: restraint, institutions, no holy war. That is the betrayal, in hardliner eyes. The reaction is a **storming of the House**: same grammar as January 6, different trigger. Martyr rage + folk-devil scapegoat + “Dole is weak / soft / stopping God’s work.” The floor fight is the open coup; after it, calm loses.

The **Stalin-level** turn is N.E.A.R. after that storm: church-state fusion, tip-line theocracy, edited Gospels, the settled peace people live inside. Same revolution, next phase — under whoever the riot installs or blesses.

**How history diverges (working):**

1. Real terms hold through Reagan's two (1981–1989).
2. **Plan 1985** drops in the second term as the quiet blueprint. Same register as Project 2025: reformers, mandates, staffing, restructuring. It takes years to implement. People who aren't watching think it is boring paperwork.
3. After 1988, Bush Sr. is president and Dan Quayle is VP. The Trump-era contingency pastiche: Reagan is made **Speaker of the House**, Bush and Quayle step down, Reagan returns through succession. Third term without a third election. The 22nd Amendment bars being *elected* more than twice; succession is the loophole.
4. The succession is **not** quietly blessed. Heavy resistance — street, press, half the country, the other party's lawyers — in the register of **Bush v. Gore (2000)**: contested legitimacy, wall-to-wall coverage, courts as the final arena. One side still wins. After the ruling (or the exhaustion), it is treated as settled law even by people who never accepted it in their gut. Story-time anniversary tapes can be regime myth *or* old fight footage, depending on who left the TV on.
5. **That** contested window is when the early purges and hard executive turn kick in — still Lenin-phase violence, unlocked by succession, not by the Plan pamphlet. Old news remembers *this* fight, not 1985 as blood year.
6. Reagan and the Moral Majority diverge: he coaxed them up; they (and Heritage / early N.E.A.R.) push past what he will bless.
7. Reagan appoints **Bob Dole** VP (25th Amendment) to stabilize after the succession trick.
8. **Assassination.** Reagan is killed. Dole inherits. Official face: metal/grunge scapegoat, tabloid-queered, **incorrect**. Manufactured outrage.
9. Dole pushes calm / institutional restraint. Hardliners read that as betrayal.
10. **Storming of the House** (Jan 6 grammar, assassination trigger — not a lost election). Coup goes open; Dole loses.
11. **Stalin-level** N.E.A.R. under whoever the storm installs or blesses. Full fusion, tip lines, scripture edit, mandatory-feeling church culture. Reagan is founder-martyr on tape / "the founders," not the living president of the late 90s / early 2000s. Dole is history's loser — erased, jailed, exiled, or rewritten soft; open.

By story time the violence is history. The revolution is already over. There is nothing left to stop. The horror is the peace that came after. How that history reaches the audience is in `medium.md`: mundanity-first shorts, then History Channel / 60 Minutes–level snippets — not a prestige prologue. **No protagonist POV for this project yet.**

Oct 2025 chat language still said MAGA / Trump for the believer's politics. In this alt-history those map onto the Reagan-N.E.A.R. machine. Diegetic branding (what people on the tapes actually say) can stay period; it does not have to say Trump.

The horror is for Christians. The regime talks like Christianity and practices the opposite. Edited scripture is load-bearing: Gospel lines on enemy-love and the immigrant are cut. A hidden "book of wisdom" / contraband verses can show up in later stories; they are not required for mundanity shorts.

## The name

N-E-A-R is the title, the regime, and the thing you pledge to. Spoken, also "Nier."

Locked expansion: **New Evangelical Apostolic Reformation**. Match the real New Apostolic Reformation as closely as a pastiche can — same N / A / R, with **Evangelical** inserted as the E. The Oct 2025 chat said "New Evangelical Apocalyptic Revolution" and, earlier the same day, "New Apocalyptic Reformation." Apocalyptic was a mistype; Revolution is superseded by Reformation.

The real-world target is the New Apostolic Reformation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Apostolic_Reformation)). N.E.A.R. pastiches and parodies it. July 2024 also tried New Era Apostolic Reformation Bible as the grassroots scripture brand. In the current world they already won: total government control, Handmaid's-Tale complete.

## Service (world texture)

Megachurch grammar. A rock band before the sermon. The pastor talks the way a pastor talks now: conversational, soft at first, then hotter. Literary scripture-voice is the wrong sound.

The sermon is about a peace they have already won. Satan is beaten. What remains gets described as leftover evil: liberals, possessed, murdering babies. Dissent is still in the news, at the tail end of being stamped out, and the news paints protests as satanic insurrection. Politics and religion are the same sentence.

The sermon should sound extreme to people who are already extreme, by stating values. It starts on ground a listener could share with Christianity, then leans harder into the values that are Christian nationalism and sin in Jesus's teaching. By the end it is the false-Christian definition, spoken as a victory.

Opener: the pledge. Closer: a rewritten Lord's Prayer, aimed at the leaders. Kevin called that closer the ultimate horror, blasphemy said casually.

Public shame is part of the service. The pastor reads a short list of people who missed church, from a place of quoted love, and asks the room to pressure them back. It is truancy in tone. It is also how the neighbor-reporting tool pays off.

Attendance is required. The world never has to say "by law." Call it service. Empty diners with the TV left on make the rule visible.

## Tip lines

Snitch and shame stay, as two numbers with different economics.

- **800 tip line** — reporting. Toll-free. Call to help a neighbor back into God's love (or request mercy for yourself). Forgiveness stays out of the copy, because forgiveness is one of the things this Bible removed. The pastor gets the name.
- **900 prayer line** — paid. Caller pays. Prayer requests sold through infomercials and advertising: late-night spots, local-news CTAs, the same grammar as psychic hotlines and miracle products. Faith as a per-minute charge.

The Oct 2025 chat had a pocket app and a photo. That form is out. The function is the same.
